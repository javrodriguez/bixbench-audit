## Proposed rule

1. **Wrong key**: no pre-registered defensible reading is accepted, **and** the reference notebook is shown, by rebuilding it from the capsule data, to compute a **different quantity** from the one the question asks (a different test, unit of observation, set or denominator).
   In-range values from readings added after the key are then listed in the note as disclosed coincidences, never hidden and never counted as support for the key.
2. **Reword**: the key is the quantity the question asks, computed under a choice the question leaves unstated (a normalisation, an estimator, a sample exclusion, a design), and at least one standard route in the field reaches it; the note names the choice and the proposed words.
3. **Undecided**: neither 1 nor 2 can be shown; the note says what would settle it.
4. A defect in the reference analysis that does not change what quantity is computed (for example bix-3's per-gene scaling) is reported as a **separate finding** and does not by itself make a key wrong.
